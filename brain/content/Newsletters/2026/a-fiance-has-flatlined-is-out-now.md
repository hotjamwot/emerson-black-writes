The third Seen in Silverbridge book is now available for purchase on Kindle and in paperback!! Magnificently exciting.

![](/organise/images/newsletters/2026/a-fiance-has-flatlined-is-out-now-01.webp)

*A Fiancé Has Flatlined* has already been called the best book of the series, and in an attempt to convince you to buy it, I want to share a review:

![](/organise/images/newsletters/2026/a-fiance-has-flatlined-is-out-now-02.webp)

How amazing is that? To be clear, I do not know this person at all. I use a NetGalley-esque service to make my books available to reviewers ahead of release, and this was their genuine review without any arm-twisting from me. I was *stoked*!

So, what are you waiting for? Support the series by giving the algorithm a kick in the right direction.

And if you don’t want to drop the $4.99 just yet, you can [add it to your ‘To Read’ shelf on Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/251352851-a-fianc-has-flatlined).

Release week is the most important time for a new release, so any support you can give me (even just clicking the link will probably help) is appreciated. To date, my author career has been a sinkhole of finances and time, and I’ve loved it. I’m obviously not doing this to make money, but I’ve let my passion for writing take precedence over marketing my work, and the books’ popularity has suffered as a result.

Artists are inherently embarrassed about trying to sell their work, and New Zealand artists even more so, but here I am, standing on my soapbox, telling you that my new book is great and you’re gonna love it.

Thank you for all your support!
