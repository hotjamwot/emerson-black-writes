How many books did you read this year? How many books did you *want* to read this year?

## 🏆 It’s award time!

This is always a tough question because book taste is so subjective. I ‘DNF’ed a lot of books this year due to not enjoying them — I even noped out of the Booker Prize winning *Orbital* after only 50 pages because I found it unbearably navel-gazy.

But hey, you’re reading this newsletter, which means you probably align with my tastes in some form, so I’ll give you my honest recommendations. Two per category.

Let’s start off with, arguably, the most boring category first.

### 🏅 Best Non-Fiction books

![The Anxious Generation by Jonathan Haidt](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-08.webp)

**The Anxious Generation** by Jonathan Haidt

Absolutely riveting. Highly recommended reading for anyone who has children in their lives, or for anyone who is keen to understand the issues facing our young people a little deeper. Definitely made me feel a lot of empathy for the poor little buggers.

![Ultra-Processed People by Chris van Tulleken](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-09.webp)

**Ultra-Processed People** by Chris van Tulleken

Quite possibly the most important and affecting book I've read in the last decade. Constantly fascinating despite being dense at times, this book not only packs a hefty punch of gobsmacking information but Chris van Tulleken's voice is so likeable and placid that it's never overwhelming.

I'd love to read it again some time.

### 🏅 Best Light Contemporary novels

![You Are Here by David Nicholls](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-10.webp)

**You Are Here** by David Nicholls

Oh man, this was so far up my alley it's ridiculous. Beautiful countryside setting, charming characters who are around my age (what a joy!) going through similar life obstacles as my peer group, and plenty of laughs.

Perfect holiday read if you've got one coming up. Or equally satisfying as a normal day read.

![Good Material by Dolly Alderton](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-11.webp)

**Good Material** by Dolly Alderton

I inhaled this book. It's probably the most fun and relatable book I've read all year.

If you want to feel what life is like for a white straight dude in his 30s, this is the book. It's a blast.

### 🏅 Best Perimenopausal Comedies

![All Fours by Miranda July](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-12.webp)

**All Fours** by Miranda July

Unexpected, graphically sexual, and often laugh-out-loud funny. Loved it.

![Big Swiss by Jen Beagin](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-13.webp)

**Big Swiss** by Jen Beagin

Darkly funny and psychologically athletic, I found this novel an uncomfortably enjoyable ride. Reading about smart, complex people who are 'messed up' from the outside is sickly appealing. Kinda like watching pimple popping videos.

### 🏅 Best Epic, Sprawling, Entire-Life-Story novels

![Demon Copperhead by Barbara Kingsolver](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-14.webp)

**Demon Copperhead** by Barbara Kingsolver

What a long, wild, painful and beautiful ride.

A modern epic, capturing a time and place that I've not experienced and yet Kingsolver somehow had me feeling nostalgia for. Twists and turns galore. Real tragedy and pain. Adrenalin rush reading at multiple chapters. A beautifully written epic.

![East of Eden by John Steinbeck](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-15.webp)

**East of Eden** by John Steinbeck

I know, I know. This book came out over seventy years ago (1952), why is it on the list? Two reasons. 1. I hadn’t read it yet and 2. They’re filming it in New Zealand right now, so I think it’s worth a mention.

A constantly entertaining and wisdom-rich classic.

The characters! The weaving plot! The setting! It's all here, captured and put on glittering show by Steinbeck, a master.

East of Eden reminded me of One Hundred Years of Solitude, with a Ken Follet twist, and some Jennifer Donnelly characters that had a hint more unpredictability to them.

It's a powerful novel that steams and slows. Perfect for a long flight or a holiday that lends itself to delving in for long uninterrupted stretches of reading. Highly recommended for anyone who loves a family epic.

## Can I choose a NUMBER ONE FAVOURITE?

No. I cannot. I thought I could, but I can’t. There were just too many great books I had the pleasure of losing myself in this year.

Some Special Mentions are:

![](/organise/images/newsletters/2024/my-favourite-books-of-2024-16.webp)

**James** by Percival Everett

**Intermezzo** by Sally Rooney

**Nomad** by Alan Partridge

**The Last Devil to Die** by Richard Osman

But as to a *favourite,* I can’t choose any single book that stood a full poppyhead taller than the rest. Too many brilliant books, it feels almost rude to pit them against each other (hence why I chose two books per category — I felt bad choosing only one).
