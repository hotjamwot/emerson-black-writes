## 🇬🇧 What else is news?

I’m glad you asked.

Stormhouse has moved to London!

![](../../../../../organise/Images/newsletters/2023/the-seen-team-is-back-02.webp)

I’ve never lived abroad. It’s something I always wanted to do but for whatever reason, I let the opportunity pass me by. Meanwhile, a bunch of my friends moved to various cities around the world and have been having the time of their lives.

When the age bracket for a NZ-UK visa changed earlier this year, I couldn’t resist any longer. So, for the next who-knows-how-long, I’ll be writing to you from London, England! Cheerio, toodle-pip, etc.

Everybody has talked a pretty big game about how *hard* it is to move overseas. Cost of living, crime, the general vibe… In general, moving your entire life to the other side of the world can seem like a gargantuan task.

I tried to put the blinkers on and just do the things I needed to do. Apply for the visa, get character references for the visa, sort through the mountains of paperwork needed to prove financial stability (of which, as a freelancer, I had precious little), book flights, sell all my possessions, yearn for some of the possessions I sold, panic about missing my friends, etc.

I tried not to worry too much. If I wanted to worry, I definitely could have. Instead, I tried to be emotionless about each task and just *do the things*.

Now that I’m here, it all seems easy. Catching the tube is a breeze, catching flights is a lot simpler than I remember, and getting a SIM card was literally effortless (they give you one with your visa that comes pre-loaded with a month of endless data).

It made me think about other things in life that we perceive to be difficult. Maybe, in reality, they won’t be so challenging. Or perhaps they *will* be challenging, but they’ll be achievable. And the more challenges we encounter and overcome, the more faith we have in our own abilities and strength moving forward.

What are some of the things you thought would be difficult but you eventually overcame? What are some things you fear will be difficult in the future? Is there anything you’re stopping yourself from doing for fear it will be too hard?

I know living in London will come with some additional challenges I’m yet to meet, but all I have to do is remember what I’ve already achieved and remind myself of my resilience.
