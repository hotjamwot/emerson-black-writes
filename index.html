About the author

Emerson Black

Emerson writes warm, witty YA mystery thrillers about young journalists uncovering corruption inside the institutions that are supposed to be running the place. The tone is affectionate rather than cynical: these are books about people who love their town and are willing to be angry on its behalf.

Originally from New Zealand, Emerson now lives in London — which is a considerable distance to maintain an accent for, and shows in the dialogue.

Beyond the fiction there's the Desk, a fortnightly note on what it's like to actually write these things: the plotting, the drafting, the parts that go wrong. If you're interested in the craft rather than the cons, that's the more honest version of this site.
